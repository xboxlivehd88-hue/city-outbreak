CITY OUTBREAK - ROAD TEXTURES

Upload road texture files into this folder.

Recommended filenames:
road_albedo.jpg      - main road/asphalt color texture
road_normal.jpg      - normal map (usually blue/purple)
road_roughness.jpg   - roughness map (usually grayscale)

Preferred size: 1024x1024.
512x512 is also fine for testing.

JPG or PNG are supported.

Do not edit index.html. Once the textures are uploaded, tell ChatGPT and the road material can be wired into the current CITY OUTBREAK build.
